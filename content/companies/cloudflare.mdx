Cloudflare operates a global edge network that accelerates and protects websites and APIs. Traffic is terminated close to users with CDN caching, load balancing, and rate limiting before it reaches origin servers. See [How Cloudflare Runs at the Edge](/case-studies/cloudflare-edge). Video platforms such as [Netflix](/companies/netflix) and [YouTube](/companies/youtube) also depend heavily on CDN and cache ideas.
