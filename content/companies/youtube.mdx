YouTube is a user-generated video platform that must ingest uploads, process many quality levels, and deliver playback worldwide. Its delivery story shares ideas with Netflix—caching, CDNs, and load balancing—while also leaning on sharding for storage and serving. Read [How YouTube Delivers Video Worldwide](/case-studies/youtube-video-delivery), and compare with [Netflix](/companies/netflix).
